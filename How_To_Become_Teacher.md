To become a school teacher in Maharashtra, you must ==complete the right academic training, pass the state eligibility exam, and apply through the official government portal==.

---

## Step 1: Choose Your Teaching Level and Get the Required Education

Your academic path depends on which classes you want to teach:

- Primary Level (Classes 1 to 5):
    
    - Pass Class 12 (HSC) with at least 50% marks.
    - Complete a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed / D.T.Ed).
    
- Upper Primary / Secondary Level (Classes 6 to 10):
    
    - Complete a Bachelor’s degree (BA, BSc, BCom) from a recognized university.
    - Complete a 2-year Bachelor of Education (B.Ed).
    
- Higher Secondary Level (Classes 11 and 12):
    
    - Earn a Post-Graduation degree in the relevant subject plus a B.Ed.
    

_Note: Proficiency in the Marathi language (reading, writing, and speaking) is mandatory._

---

## Step 2: Qualify the Maharashtra Teacher Eligibility Test (MAHA TET)

To teach in government, local body (Zilla Parishad), or private-aided schools in Maharashtra, you must pass the [Maharashtra State Council of Examination](https://mahatet.in/) (MSCE) test:

- Paper I: For teaching Classes 1 to 5 (requires D.El.Ed).
- Paper II: For teaching Classes 6 to 8 (requires B.Ed).
- Alternatively, qualifying the central CTET is also accepted for many schools.
- The MAHA TET certificate has a lifetime validity.

---

## Step 3: Register and Apply via the Pavitra Portal

Maharashtra conducts centralized teacher recruitment for government and aided schools through a digital portal:

- Pavitra Portal (Teacher Aptitude and Intelligence Test - TAIT):
    
    - You must register and appear for the TAT / TAIT exam conducted by the state education department when notifications drop.
    - Update your qualification details, MAHA TET/CTET score, and TAIT score on the official Pavitra Portal.
    - School management committees or Zilla Parishads select candidates from the portal merit lists for Shikshan Sevak (contractual teaching) or permanent teacher positions.
    

---

## Step 4: Alternative Path for Private / Un-Aided Schools

If you want to work in private or international schools that do not rely on state council recruitment:

- Apply directly to schools with your resume, degree certificates (B.Ed/D.El.Ed), and TET/CTET scores (though some private schools may waive TET).
- Clear a personal interview and a live demo-teaching session.
