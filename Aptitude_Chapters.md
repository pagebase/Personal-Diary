## Quantitative Aptitude (Mathematics)

**Arithmetic (The Foundation)**

* Percentages, Profit, Loss, and Discount
* Simple and Compound Interest
* Ratio, Proportion, and Partnership
* Averages and Problems on Ages
* Time, Speed, and Distance (including Problems on Trains, Boats, and Streams)
* Time and Work, Pipes and Cisterns
* Mixtures and Alligations

**Algebra & Number System**

* Number System (HCF, LCM, Divisibility Rules, Remainders, Cyclicity)
* Linear and Quadratic Equations
* Surds, Indices, Exponents, and Logarithms

**Geometry & Mensuration**

* 2D and 3D Mensuration (Area, Volume, Surface Area)
* Lines, Angles, Triangles, Circles, and Polygons
* Trigonometry Basics and Heights & Distances

**Modern Math & Data Interpretation**

* Permutation and Combination
* Probability
* Data Interpretation (Tables, Bar Charts, Pie Charts, Line Graphs)
